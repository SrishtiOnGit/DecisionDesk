# DecisionDesk

**Think better. Decide better.**

DecisionDesk is a web app that helps people make decisions without overthinking themselves into oblivion.

Whether you're choosing between two projects, deciding what to buy, or trying to figure out your next move, DecisionDesk helps organize your options in one place instead of opening 17 browser tabs and asking the same question to 5 different people (we've all been there 😭).

---

## Why I Built This

Fun fact: this project started because I couldn't decide what project to build.

I kept bouncing between ideas, comparing them, overthinking them, and somehow spending more time deciding than actually building.

That's when I realized a lot of decisions aren't hard because we lack information — they're hard because our thoughts are scattered.

So I decided to build a tool that helps people structure their thinking before making a choice.

And yes... the app that helps people make decisions was born from my inability to make one.

## Current Status

DecisionDesk is currently in active development.

Right now, the focus is building a solid foundation and understanding React concepts while creating something genuinely useful.

## Future Vision

My goal is to turn DecisionDesk into more than just a random-choice generator.

I want it to become a place where people can evaluate options logically, reflect on their choices, and make decisions with confidence.

Because sometimes the hardest part isn't finding the answer.

It's organizing the question.
